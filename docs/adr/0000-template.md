## Context

What forces are at play: the problem, the constraints, and what we know.
Spell out acronyms on first use.

## Decision

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we will have to watch.
Include the conditions under which we would revisit the decision.

## Alternatives considered
